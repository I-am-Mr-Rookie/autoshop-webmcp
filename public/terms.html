A demonstration, not a store

AutoShop shows how a seller can let a browser agent act within limits. Nothing is for sale. Orders, stock, and receipts are synthetic, and no goods, money, or payments change hands.

Acceptable use

  • Use the demo to explore the buyer and seller flows, by hand or with a WebMCP-capable agent.
  • Do not attempt to access another account's shop, disrupt the service, or overload it with automated traffic.
  • Do not enter real personal, payment, or confidential information into order fields.

No warranty

The demo is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It may change, reset its data, or go offline at any time. The source code is published under the license in the project repository.
