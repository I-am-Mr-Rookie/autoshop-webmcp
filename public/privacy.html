Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-21

What this is

AutoShop is a hackathon demonstration of bounded human–agent authority. Products, stock, orders, mandates, and receipts are synthetic. No payment is ever requested or processed.

What we collect

  • Sign-in. You sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email. A third-party authentication service handles that sign-in and holds your email address and basic profile. AutoShop's server receives only a verified account ID per request.
  • Demo shop data. Your account ID is turned into a hashed database schema name (shop_<sha256 prefix>). That schema holds only the synthetic carts, orders, mandates, approvals, and receipts you create.
  • Cookies. Strictly necessary session cookies for sign-in, plus one seller-session cookie while you use the seller control plane. No advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Logs. AutoShop's server logs generic error events, not request bodies. Our hosting provider, Railway, may keep standard request logs such as IP address and path.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, run ads, add third-party analytics, or ask for passwords. Text you type into orders is treated as untrusted content and never authorizes an action.

Retention and removal

Demo data may be reset or deleted at any time while the demonstration runs. To ask for your sign-in account or demo shop to be removed, open an issue on the project repository without posting personal details, and we will follow up.

See also the terms of use.
